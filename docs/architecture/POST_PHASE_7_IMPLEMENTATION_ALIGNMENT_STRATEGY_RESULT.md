# Post-Phase-7 Implementation Alignment Strategy Result

## 1. Executive Alignment Strategy

DayFrame should preserve its deterministic scheduling substrate—canonical user-days, Work/cycle expansion, Commitment recurrence, Preview of authorized facts, Friction/Suggested Fix correction, exact decision replay, immutable publication, execution/Progress separation, Month/Today/Summary read models, and versioned persistence—while adding the missing planning semantics as first-class domains. Goal Structure, Goal Demand, Demand Projection, Capacity, Feasibility, Competing Demand, Allocation, Constructive Proposal, ProposalDecision, Accepted Allocation, Commitment Composition, and Live Opportunity must not be simulated by stretching current candidates, openings, links, fixes, or decisions.

Current Goal, Commitment, schedule, execution, and history identities form useful roots, but new revisioned authority/provenance seams and migrations are required. Preview remains projection, Friction remains corrective, Goals remain outcomes, and Commitments remain time-owning authority. Saved Setup Profiles should retire as a primary product concept with compatibility; backups remain foundational. Architecture is sufficiently complete. Select **Path A — Implementation Roadmap** without beginning it here.

## 2. Scope

This strategy determines what to preserve, extend, adapt, replace, retire, migrate, introduce, correct, polish, or defer. It does not order implementation, create a roadmap, name a phase, change code, or reopen completed architecture/reproduction work.

## 3. Relationship to Earlier Alignment Strategy

The earlier strategy is `docs/alignment/Implementaion_Alignment_Strategy.md`. It addressed the original Engine/Interaction architecture and audit baseline, establishing architecture authority, epistemic/lifecycle integrity, user authority, recovery, continuity, coherent increments, and evidence-first decisions. Its recommendations led to the current surface boundaries, durable authority, Preview/Friction/decision/publication/execution foundations, and test discipline.

Those durable principles remain. Its earlier lifecycle assumptions are superseded where accepted post-Phase-7 specifications now define Goal Structure, Demand, Capacity, Composition, constructive Proposal, Accepted Allocation, and Live Opportunity. The file and its misspelled historical name remain unchanged.

## 4. Governing Evidence

Normative: `POST_PHASE_7_ARCHITECTURE_SYNTHESIS_RESULT.md`; accepted Capacity, Goal Demand/Allocation, Goal Structure, Commitment Composition, and Constructive Proposal results; incorporated execution, Progress, history, Friction, publication, recurrence, user-day, provenance, and decision architecture.

## 5. Evidence Model

Accepted architecture is **Intended Truth**; current production/tests are **Implemented Truth**; Dogfood is **Experienced Truth**. Statements are Confirmed, Inferred, or Not Found. Existing code never becomes normative merely by existing.

## 6. Target Architecture Summary

Teach authors constraints, Commitments/composition, Goals/structure/Demand/Priority, availability policy, and preferences. Plan derives Capacity, feasibility, competition, Allocation, and Proposal; explicit decisions create bounded Accepted Allocation; realization creates time authority. Preview projects authority, publication freezes it, Live executes it and derives opportunities from divergence, and Learn interprets immutable history without silently authorizing behavior.

## 7. Current Implementation Summary

Confirmed current foundations include shift/cycle Work generation; Commitment templates/recurrences; fixed/flexible placement; manual events; Preview/Friction/Suggested Fix; PlanDecision and Accepted Choices; publication; Today, Month, Review Schedule, Summary; execution records/corrections; Goal identity/links; measurements/Progress observations; historical plans/intelligence; profiles/backups; versioned persistence and migrations. Missing are the synthesized demand-to-allocation chain, Goal Structure, Composition, constructive Proposal, Live Opportunity, reusable preference promotion, and corresponding history.

## 8. Dogfood Experienced-Truth Summary

Dogfood validated major primitives but exposed buried Work/Buffer/Goal workflows, confusing rotations and off-day policy, overloaded planning range, conflict/choice scalability, missing Capacity/Proposal/composition, disconnected operating surfaces, profile ambiguity, and provenance/visual weaknesses. Every ledger item is routed in section 47.

## 9. DF-006 Final Disposition

Carry exactly: **DF006-RC7 — Historical Defect Confirmed, Exact Technical Layer Unresolved**; **BR5 — Historical Observation Confirmed, Current Reproduction Not Achieved**; **S2**; **DF006-FINAL-C2 — Historical Defect Confirmed and Bounded**. Do not invent a fix. Preserve `DF006-REG-01`–`17`: weekday/manual-cycle correctness, range/week-start invariance, saved/draft and stale/current clarity, exact Work provenance, and Month evidence placement.

## 10. Architectural Dependency Chain

Current Goal identity begins the target chain; direct Goal links skip the absent Structure/Demand/Capacity/Allocation/Proposal stages. Candidate/opening/placement mechanisms approximate geometry, not semantics. PlanDecision/publication/execution/Progress/history resume the chain after authority already exists. Alignment must fill the middle with distinct versioned domains and explicit transitions. Composition feeds Commitment footprint/liability before Capacity; Live divergence feeds Released Interval/Live Capacity before the same Proposal lifecycle.

## 11. Authority Hierarchy Alignment

Hard authored Commitments, Work, events, schedule authority, explicit decisions, and recurrence exist. Capacity policy, Demand constraints, Goal Priority, Allocation Policy, reusable preferences, and learned tendencies do not exist as a complete hierarchy. Current template `priority` is placement order, not Goal Priority. Accepted Choices are historical corrective authority, not reusable rules. Introduce typed authority levels and prevent heuristics/storage order from crossing them.

## 12. Epistemic-State Alignment

Authored state, Preview-derived state, corrective choices, publication, execution, Progress, and history are substantially distinct. Missing boundaries are constructive Proposal → ProposalDecision → Accepted Allocation → realization, plus learned tendency → explicit preference promotion. Flexible recurring placement is realization of prior authority, not Proposal. Goal links are provenance, not Demand.

## 13. Commitment Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve source/incarnation identity, template/recurrence, fixed/flexible behavior, manual events, Work specialization, occurrence identity, placement, publication, and execution. Add explicit commitment priority semantics, lifecycle/provenance extensions, composition roles, liabilities, direct scheduled Goal work differentiation, and richer policy. Avoid replacing the sound base type.

## 14. Goal Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve UUID identity, lifecycle, links, measurement definitions, observations, Summary/history projections. Add outcome intent fields only where specified, distinct Goal Priority, Structure, Demand, and direct spontaneous/scheduled service provenance. A Goal-linked Commitment never becomes Demand automatically.

## 15. Goal Structure Alignment

**Introduce.** Add revisioned typed relationships (`contains`, `contributesTo`, `dependsOn`) and Milestone authority with exact endpoints, lifecycle, eligibility, and frozen history. Existing Goal links cannot represent structure and should remain service association.

## 16. Goal Demand Alignment

**Introduce.** Add authored versioned Demand Intent and disposable bounded Demand Projection with effort, horizon, cadence/session shape, minimum/target/optional satisfaction, constraints, structural normalization, and advisory Progress pressure. `BlockRecurrence` and `BlockCandidate` are not Demand.

## 17. Capacity Alignment

**Introduce**, reusing low-level interval geometry. Capacity is canonical-user-day, demand-neutral, policy-qualified, liability-aware derived interval truth with freshness, coverage, provenance, and reason codes. `findOpenings`/placement gaps remain helpers; an opening alone is not allocatable Capacity.

## 18. Commitment Composition Alignment

**Introduce**, while adapting existing Buffer and relative-placement helpers. Add Attachment Relationship, attached occurrence pairing, Composite Occurrence/Footprint/Liability, required/optional transactionality, CompositeDecision, and separate execution for real support activities. Buffers protect but never execute. `beforeWork`/`afterWork` remain useful window heuristics until explicit relationship authority replaces overloaded use.

## 19. Proposal Alignment

**Introduce.** Preview remains authorized projection; Suggested Fix remains corrective. Build deterministic Proposal/No-Proposal over Capacity, Demand, feasibility, Allocation, policy, and guidance. ProposalDecision validates explicit response; Accepted Allocation is new bounded authority; realization alone creates schedule facts.

## 20. Live Opportunity / Found Time Alignment

**Introduce**, reusing Today, published-plan, execution timing/outcome, and user-day primitives. Derive Released Interval from divergence, subtract remaining liabilities into Live Capacity, create expiring Live Opportunity, and use the same Proposal lifecycle. Direct spontaneous Goal execution remains parallel. A native timer is optional.

## 21. Friction Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve deterministic detection, explanations, bounded Suggested Fix, acceptance, applicability, and decision replay. Add composition liabilities, richer provenance, scoped/bulk corrective workflows, and scalable review. Never use Friction for pre-authority competition or constructive allocation.

## 22. Decision Model Alignment

**Adapt shared infrastructure; keep semantic records distinct.** Preserve identity, replay, validation, persistence, and stale checks. PlanDecision corrects occurrences; CompositeDecision changes composite authority; ProposalDecision responds to recommendation; Accepted Allocation owns bounded resource authorization. Shared envelopes must not erase type-specific invariants.

## 23. Accepted Choice / Learning Alignment

**Adapt.** Preserve persisted visible decision evidence and applicability. Add history-resolution/scoping and explicit promotion into separate reusable preference authority. Introduce learned tendency as non-authoritative interpretation. Accepted repetition never becomes Priority or rule automatically.

## 24. Publication Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve immutable HistoricalPlan/publication, exact user-day context, source/occurrence provenance, and stale rejection. Add Scheduled Goal Work, support activities, Buffer protection, Accepted Allocation/Proposal lineage, composition snapshots, Capacity claims, and legacy origin. Unaccepted Proposal content cannot publish.

## 25. Execution Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve completed/partial/skipped, actual duration, correction/retraction/restoration, planned-vs-actual, and historical targeting. Add cancellation/early completion where missing, composite support subjects, Accepted Allocation lineage, direct spontaneous Goal execution, and released-interval derivation. Timer remains deferred.

## 26. Progress Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve measurement definitions, explicit observations, manual quantity progress, correction/retraction, and Goal history. Add explicit attribution from execution/spontaneous action and milestone/outcome policy. Scheduled, accepted, or elapsed effort never automatically equals Progress.

## 27. History Alignment

**Extend/Adapt.** Preserve immutable publications, executions, observations, decisions, source snapshots, legacy origins, and corrections. Add Proposal/Decision/Allocation, Demand/Capacity context, Structure/composition revisions, pairing, liability, and learning provenance. Separate operational, analytic, and archival resolution without rewriting old meaning.

## 28. Month Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve accessible grid, canonical day cells, current/stale coverage, exact navigation, Work/events/Commitments/Friction. Add Goal work, Proposal attention scoped by Proposal Horizon, Capacity summaries, composite evidence, and provenance. Separate Planning-Data Horizon, Proposal Horizon, and Review Scope. Carry DF-006 evidence assertions.

## 29. Review Schedule Alignment

**Adapt.** Keep authorized schedule review, Friction, corrective fixes, Accepted Choices, and publication readiness. Show constructive Proposals as distinct objects or route to their owning decision workspace; never conflate them with Preview. Add bounded scopes, grouping, and scalable resolution.

## 30. Today / Live Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve current published user-day, obligations, execution/outcome reporting, and exact identities. Add divergence, Released Interval, Live Capacity, Live Opportunity, expiring Proposal/decision, direct spontaneous Goal action, and bounded rescheduling.

## 31. Summary Alignment

**Extend.** Preserve execution, Progress, Goal, history, and schedule-realization basics. Add Capacity context, Demand/allocation/proposal outcomes, composition, accepted decisions where explanatory, and non-authoritative learning. Separate operational, analytic, and archive scale.

## 32. Teach Alignment

**Adapt.** Work Pattern owns Work/cycles; Commitment Library owns Commitment/recurrence/composition; Goal authoring owns Structure/Demand/Priority; scheduling preferences own user-day/availability/promotion policies. Expose Buffers/attachments and effective authority without burying them. This establishes ownership, not screen design.

## 33. Saved Setup Profiles Alignment

**Retire with compatibility** as a primary generalized operating concept. Preserve stored profile data and import/read during a bounded compatibility period; do not confuse it with backups. Future templates, scenarios, or reusable patterns require separately specified semantics rather than renaming profiles. Backups remain Preserve/Extend.

## 34. Persistence / Migration Alignment

Use additive versioned stores for new authority and disposable recomputation for derived Capacity/Demand Projection/feasibility/Allocation where architecture permits. Persist identity-bearing authored, accepted, scheduled, executed, historical, and decision records. Migrate semantic fields explicitly; preserve quarantines/recovery and atomic backup composition.

## 35. Legacy Compatibility

Old shifts/cycles/templates/recurrences/events/Goals retain identity. Old Goal links remain service provenance, not inferred Demand. Existing decisions remain historical corrective decisions. Published occurrences remain immutable. Unsupported origins become `legacyUnknown`, never silently retargeted. Old profiles/backups restore through versioned adapters or protected recovery; UI removal cannot precede read/migration support.

## 36. Planning Range Separation

Current Preview range combines engine data loading, generation, and user review. **Adapt** it into explicit Planning-Data Horizon, Proposal Horizon, and Review Scope. Broad data may support narrow proposals/review; changing review scope must not regenerate semantics, and proposal generation must not expand merely because annual data is loaded.

## 37. Priority Alignment

Keep Commitment Priority, Goal Priority, and decision/preference priority separate. Existing template `priority` may migrate into Commitment protection/placement semantics only after mapping; it cannot seed Goal Priority. Allocation consumes Goal Priority and policy; reusable guidance ranking is lower authority.

## 38. Provenance Alignment

Extend durable identity infrastructure to distinguish recurring authority, direct scheduled, Proposal-accepted, Found-Time-accepted, corrective decision, direct spontaneous, and `legacyUnknown`. Every Goal/Demand/Capacity claim/feasibility/allocation/proposal/composition/execution/Progress relation carries exact identity/revision/input fingerprint and historical resolution.

## 39. Freshness Alignment

Reuse Preview’s dependency-aware stale pattern, not its single boolean. Each derived domain needs typed dependency fingerprints, coverage, validity horizon, and stale/unknown/inapplicable states. Acceptance and publication revalidate decisive inputs. DF-006 requires current-vs-draft authority and source evidence to remain visible.

## 40. Determinism Alignment

Capacity, Demand Projection, feasibility, competition, Allocation, Proposal ranking/No-Proposal, Live Capacity, Found-Time Proposal, provenance, and migrations must yield equivalent semantic output/order for equivalent inputs. Stable tie-breakers replace storage order. LLMs may explain structured results but never supply scheduling authority.

## 41. Explainability Alignment

Reuse typed Friction reasons, applicability, identities, and decision evidence. Introduce structured reason codes for Capacity exclusion/liability, Demand qualification, feasibility, competition, allocation, proposal ranking/No-Proposal, freshness, and migration. Rendering may summarize but must retain exact inspectable provenance.

## 42. Test Architecture Alignment

Preserve current user-day, recurrence, cycle, placement, Friction, decision, publication, execution, Progress, history, migration, and surface suites. Adapt Preview/Goal-link/profile tests around new boundaries; retire only tests asserting retired profile UX or overloaded semantics after compatibility coverage exists. Introduce semantic suites for Structure, Demand, Capacity, feasibility, competition, Allocation, Proposal/No-Proposal/Decision/Accepted Allocation, composition/liability, Live Opportunity, provenance/freshness, realization/publication, history/migration, and DF-006 REG-01–17.
